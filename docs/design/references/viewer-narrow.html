T05 · Viewer composition · Narrow and tablet

One board, every width, no horizontal page scroll

At narrow widths the filter strip becomes a summary and a drawer, blocks stack in one column, and charts keep their summary and data table. The 320 px composition is the minimum, not a squeezed desktop. The tablet composition keeps filters inline and uses the 8-column grid.

320 px Header wraps into identity and actions. Metrics become one column. Chart axes and legend remain readable; the table alternative is available.
390 px Two metric columns fit. Filter summary and mode controls stay reachable without covering the board title.
Tablet At 768 px the filter strip can stay inline and the 8-column grid gives the chart five columns with a three-column side panel.
