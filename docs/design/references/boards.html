T05 · Board range

One visual language, three different working boards

An assessment overview, a commercial metrics board and an operational live-monitoring board share tokens, typography and state treatment while changing their composition. They are design references, not invented product features or real data sources.

Assessment Comparison-led. Prior-year reference, school table and a district narrative. See viewer desktop.
Commercial Margin-led. A wide performance chart, a compact side comparison and a channel detail table.
Operational Time-led. Live status, queue health, incident rail and a dense event table. Simulated transport is labeled visibly.
